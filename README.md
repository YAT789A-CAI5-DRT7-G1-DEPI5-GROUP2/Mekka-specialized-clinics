Project Name : AI-Powered Digital Marketing Strategy for Nasayem Mekka-specialized-clinics

Project Idea :

This project aims to develop an integrated digital marketing strategy for Makkah Specialized Clinics using Artificial Intelligence in Digital Arts.

The strategy focuses on creating valuable medical awareness content, AI-generated videos, engaging visual designs, and promotional campaigns to strengthen the clinic's digital presence.

The project aims to increase audience engagement, grow social media followers, improve awareness of the clinic's medical services, and encourage more patients to visit the clinics.

All medical awareness content were reviewed for accuracy before publication.

Work Plan :

Phase 1: Research
Analyze the clinic's services, target audience, and current digital presence.

Phase 2: Strategy
Define marketing objectives, content pillars, communication style, and KPIs.

Phase 3: Content Planning
Develop a content calendar for medical awareness and promotional campaigns.

Phase 4: AI Content Production
Create AI-assisted videos, medical awareness visuals, and promotional designs.

Phase 5: Publishing and Promotion
Prepare content for social media publishing and promotional activities.

Phase 6: Performance Evaluation
Review engagement, follower growth, reach, and available patient-visit data.

Roles & Responsibilities :

Project Leader
Coordinate project activities and monitor progress.
Ensure consistency between the marketing strategy and project objectives.
Review deliverables and coordinate team responsibilities.

AI Video Production
Develop concepts and scripts for short-form videos.
Use AI tools to support video creation and visual storytelling.
Maintain consistent branding and production quality.

Graphic Design
Create social media posts, promotional visuals, and campaign designs.
Follow the clinic's visual identity and brand guidelines.
Prepare visual assets for different social media formats.

Content Writing and Social Media
Write medical awareness content and promotional captions.
Develop content calendars and calls to action.
Adapt content to the target audience and platform.

Performance Analysis
Track social media performance using available analytics.
Compare results with the project's KPIs.
Prepare recommendations for future campaigns.

Responsibilities are combined between all team members.

KPIs :

Follower Growth
Measure growth in the clinic's social media audience.

Engagement Rate
Evaluate audience interaction with published content.

Content Reach
Measure the number of people reached by the content.

Video Views
Evaluate the visibility of AI-generated videos.

Patient Inquiries
Measure interest in the clinic's services.

Clinic Visits
Evaluate changes in patient attendance.

Performance will be evaluated by comparing results before and after the campaign, while considering campaign duration, publishing frequency, paid advertising, and other relevant factors.
